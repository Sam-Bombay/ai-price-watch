The honest caveat

Cheap is not the same as equivalent. The cheapest models in this table are small, fast and fine for classification, extraction, drafting and support replies; they are not what you write a long-context agent on. The point is not "use the cheapest model" — it is that most companies are paying premium rates for the routine share of their traffic, and the routine share is usually most of it. Splitting traffic by task is where the money is.
