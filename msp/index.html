What the pack contains

1. The audit method

An eight-line worksheet that takes a client's AI and software spend and returns three numbers: spend today, spend after the cuts, and what has to be re-priced because the vendor changed the price. Runs on a CSV export — no client credentials, no screen sharing.

2. The client report

A one-page template in your branding: the cuts, the dated evidence for each, and the decision the client has to make. Written to be forwarded to a CFO, not read aloud in a meeting.

3. The live price feed

Every model price change, logged daily, with an embeddable chart you can put on your own site or in your monthly newsletter. Each entry is dated — so a saving you claim is checkable, which is the difference between a report and an opinion.

4. The escalation path

When a client's bill is big enough to be worth the deep work, I do the audit behind you. You keep the relationship, the account and your margin; I stay invisible.
