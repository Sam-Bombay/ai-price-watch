Why the timing matters

The model market repriced twice in the last 24 hours. On 16 Oct one model's input price fell 92.5%; on 17 Oct the same input rose 1,227%. If your bill was set on last quarter's numbers, it is wrong — in one direction or the other.

What the audit actually looks at

  • Tier mismatch. Enterprise or flat-rate plans sitting under usage a free or usage-only tier already covers. This is usually the biggest single line.
  • Model routing. Paying frontier prices for classification, extraction, summarising and formatting — work that a model at a tenth of the price does indistinguishably.
  • Zombie seats. Licences attached to people who left, or to a workflow that was replaced six months ago.
  • Duplicate tooling. Two products doing the same job because two teams bought separately.
  • Renewal terms. Auto-renew clauses, seat minimums, and annual commitments that no longer match headcount.
