Self-host, or keep paying per token?

Computed 2026-11-05 from 389 hosted models with 100k+ context, against published GPU rental rates. Two workloads, two different answers.

The short answer: it depends on whether your load is batchable.

A 24/7 user-facing product pays for the GPU whether or not anyone is typing, so it only wins above roughly $1,307/month of hosted spend. Batch and overnight work pays for GPU-hours only, and wins almost immediately. Most AI bills are a mix and nobody has separated the two — which is exactly where the money is.
