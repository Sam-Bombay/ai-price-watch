Normalize annual and monthly bills, include migration costs, and calculate a 30% fee only on positive aggregate net savings. This is arithmetic, not a claim that a cheaper service matches your requirements. Validate quality, volume, taxes, credits and invoice evidence first.

3. Recover missed enquiries, not consent

For appointment-led services: turn an existing CSV into a human review queue. Only open leads, explicit consent marked yes, and at least seven days since contact qualify. Closed, opted-out and recent leads are excluded. Duplicate IDs and future dates are rejected. No messages are generated or sent.

Download offline tool · Fictional lead fixture

python3 money_tools.py leads leads-example.csv --as-of 2026-10-18

Use anonymous IDs. Staff look up the original enquiry and recheck lawful contact permissions. A yes column is not legal proof. One manual follow-up, no bulk sequence. The fixture returns one review candidate, three exclusions, zero messages sent.
