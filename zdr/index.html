What was published, dated

  • 24 Nov 2026 — OpenRouter's notice that requests to MiniMax models "may have been read". A researcher embedded unique URLs in prompts; a third party opened them 40 to 130 hours later. It happened on non-MiniMax providers too: AtlasCloud, Novita and StreamLake told OpenRouter they had forwarded OpenRouter requests for MiniMax models to MiniMax.
  • 25 Nov 2026 — a public trace of provider traffic (kendell.dev/blog/arkwrapper) reports DeepSeek tokens on routes traced to ByteDance Ark through Novita, StreamLake and Ant Digital, plus StreamLake returning Novita gateway errors and tool-call ids on GLM 5.3 Flash, and SiliconFlow passing through StreamLake request ids on DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, V4 Pro and GLM 5.
  • 22 Nov 2026 — OpenRouter off-boarded three providers and removed 125 endpoints from zero-data-retention eligibility. The list of those 125 was never published.
  • What is still not public — a per-route retention flag. Nothing on this page claims a route is compliant, private or breached. It shows who serves you and what the alternative costs.

Method

Routes: openrouter.ai/api/v1/models/<id>/endpoints, fetched 27 November 2026, 455 models, 80 distinct providers. Prices: the 27 November 2026 catalogue snapshot, the same one the log diffs daily (1,475 dated events since 8 October). Delta = cheapest route not named in a report, minus cheapest route overall, on the 100/20M workload. A negative delta means the unnamed route is cheaper. Report facts are quoted from their published sources, not modelled.
